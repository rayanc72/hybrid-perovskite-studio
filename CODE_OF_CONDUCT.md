# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or career stage.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give scientific disagreements appropriate evidence and context.
- Respect privacy and do not share unpublished data without permission.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work toward correction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository's contact channels. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and addressed proportionately, including warnings, removal of contributions, or exclusion from project spaces when necessary.
